TaxiMeterCore 0.8.11 — partial-GPS fare estimate and 1,000-toman rounding

- Always apply the configured minimum/base fare before rounding calculated fares.
- Round calculated fare estimates to the nearest 1,000 toman (half-up).
- If GPS coverage is incomplete, calculate an estimate from allocated/valid GPS distance rather than discarding the estimate.
- Keep the trip marked for review when a GPS gap exists; the unknown distance is not fabricated or charged as known distance.
- Show the valid recorded distance and GPS-derived estimate in the review dialog, allowing the driver to confirm or adjust it.
- Preserve the GPS-derived estimate and record manual changes through the existing adjustment/audit trail.
- Update fare-calculation unit tests for the new rounding and partial-GPS behavior.
